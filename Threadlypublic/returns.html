THREADLY RETURNS

Returns Made Simple

We want you to feel confident about every purchase. If something isn't right, here's how returns work at THREADLY.

↩
RETURN POLICY

Before You Return

Items may be returned if they are eligible under THREADLY's return conditions.

  • Items should be unused and in their original condition.
  • Clothing should still have its original tags where applicable.
  • Shoes should be returned without damage or excessive wear.
  • Items should be returned with their original packaging where possible.
✓
THE PROCESS

How Returns Work

01

Contact Us

Contact THREADLY through our Help & Support page and tell us about the item you want to return.

02

Return Approval

Our team will review the request and provide the necessary return instructions.

03

Send the Item

Once your return is approved, follow the instructions provided by THREADLY to send the item back.

04

Return Completed

After the returned item has been received and checked, the next step will be handled according to the return outcome.

!
ELIGIBILITY

Items That May Not Qualify

Used Items

Items that show signs of significant use or damage may not qualify for a return.

Damaged Items

Items damaged after delivery may not be accepted as a standard return.

Missing Packaging

Where original packaging is required, missing packaging may affect return eligibility.

Non-Eligible Items

Certain products may have specific return conditions. Contact us if you are unsure.

i

Need help with a return?

If you're unsure whether your item qualifies for a return, contact us before sending anything back.
