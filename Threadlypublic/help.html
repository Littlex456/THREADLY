Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common THREADLY questions.

How do I place an order?

Browse the THREADLY shop, select a product, choose your preferred size, and add it to your cart. Continue to checkout when you are ready to place your order.

What payment methods are available?

THREADLY currently supports the payment methods made available during checkout. Follow the instructions shown on the payment page after placing your order.

How do I know if my payment was confirmed?

After submitting your payment receipt, THREADLY will verify the payment. You can check the payment status through the order process and contact support if verification is taking longer than expected.

How can I track my order?

Open My Orders from your account or menu. Select the order you want to view to see its current delivery status and tracking information.

What if my order is delayed?

First check the delivery status in My Orders. If you need more information, contact THREADLY support using one of the contact options below.

Can I contact THREADLY about my order?

Yes. You can contact the THREADLY support team through WhatsApp, email, or phone. Keep your order number nearby so we can assist you faster.
